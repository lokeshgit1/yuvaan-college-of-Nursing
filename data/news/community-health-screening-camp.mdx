As part of their rural community health posting, **GNM** and **ANM** students organised a full-day preventive screening and health education camp in collaboration with local Primary Health Centre staff.

## Services Provided During the Camp

Over **180 community residents** received preventive check-ups and one-on-one health education under faculty supervision:

- **Blood Pressure & Blood Glucose Screening:** Early identification and referral advice for adults with elevated readings.
- **Maternal & Child Nutrition Desk:** Visual demonstrations on iron-rich local diets, antenatal check-up schedules, and childhood immunisation tracking.
- **Hygiene & Seasonal Illness Prevention:** Interactive poster sessions with local school children on handwashing and safe drinking water practices.

Community postings remain a cornerstone of training at Yuvaan College of Nursing, helping student nurses understand social determinants of health beyond hospital walls.
