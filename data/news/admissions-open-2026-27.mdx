Yuvaan College of Nursing (Mahura, Tamando, Khordha) invites applications from aspiring healthcare students for the **2026–27 academic session** across our **GNM** and **ANM** nursing diploma programmes.

## Programmes Accepting Pre-Enquiries

- **General Nursing & Midwifery — GNM (3 Years)** — For 10+2 candidates meeting INC/State eligibility
- **Auxiliary Nurse Midwifery — ANM (2 Years)** — Primary & maternal healthcare diploma after 10+2 (60 seats)

## How to Begin Your Application

1. **Submit an Online Enquiry:** Use our quick enquiry form so an academic counsellor can clarify eligibility, seat availability, and hostel options.
2. **Download the Prospectus & Admission Form:** Review the semester fee schedule and document checklist in our downloadable PDFs.
3. **Visit Campus for Counselling:** Parents and students are welcome Monday to Saturday (9:00 AM – 5:00 PM) for a guided tour of our Nursing Foundation Lab and library.

> **Important Note:** All admissions are strictly merit- and eligibility-based in accordance with Indian Nursing Council (INC), Odisha Nurses & Midwives Registration Council (ONMRC), and State Counselling guidelines.
